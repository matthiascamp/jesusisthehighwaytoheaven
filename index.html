Author

Edwin "Lucky" Lewis

Author · Witness for Christ

I am Edwin Lewis, the younger brother of rugby league legend Wally Lewis. I once had a promising football career of my own — so promising that even my parents believed I had the potential to surpass my famous brother. But a devastating car accident changed everything.

I had just started an apprentice panel beating and spray painting job at Coorparoo United Body Works — I was due back at work the following Monday. That night, my two mates from Brisbane State High — Stephen and Brett — had a flat battery in their car. I swapped my battery into Stephen's car so they could get going, and pushed my car with his dead battery in it. Before I left, Mark Skelton — a member of the surf club and the boyfriend of John Privatero's sister — asked me to get two boys, Gary and Peter, home safely. Mark wanted to stay the night with his girlfriend. I gave him my word: I will get them home safely.

So I followed Stephen's car with Brett in the passenger seat, and we turned off at the Beenleigh exit on the old Gold Coast route to Brisbane. In the old days, we used to salute the soldier at Yatala for a safe trip home. I cannot remember if we saluted him that day.

The driver coming towards us had been drinking all day — morning session and afternoon session, as they did on Sundays in those days. He had already lost his licence three times for drunk driving. My cousin Paul saw this man stumbling, falling over, and then getting into his car blind drunk. He could barely stand. Paul's testimony meant nothing to the police — they couldn't have cared less.

That driver lost control, just avoided Stephen and Brett's car in front of me, and then hit me head-on. Gary was killed instantly — thrown straight out of the back window. Peter was brought to hospital still living, but he died one day later.

I died multiple times. At the scene I stopped breathing, and by God's providence a nurse happened to be passing by in a car. She slit my throat and placed a straw in my windpipe to keep me alive. From the accident site in Marsden to the PA Hospital, my mother and father were later told by the ambulance crew that I had to be resuscitated seven times — and potentially more times after that. From having my throat cut and the straw, I sustained five broken ribs and two collapsed lungs — and these were only a minor part of the total injuries I incurred.

I was in a coma for nine days. Nobody could get me out. Wally came to my bedside and started telling me he was going down to the surf club the next day — Boxing Day — and how many beers he was going to have for me. I didn't respond. But then he said he'd have a couple of rumbos for me — and that's when I acknowledged. My left arm was the only thing not broken, and I squeezed his hand. That squeeze let them know I was okay.

When I woke, I couldn't speak. I had to communicate like Skippy: one click for no, two clicks for yes, and many clicks for a big yes. The Lord said, you've got to revert back to Skippy. And so I did, learning to live again one click at a time. Months and months went past, and then I was told the two boys had died.

My parents, no matter what, just kept me living. Wally was overseas in Austria when he was notified that I was dead — or close to it. He came back. That is when Wally's involvement, and the story told in his book, begins.

I gave Mark Skelton my word that I would get those boys home safely. I carry that with me. But through it all, God had a greater purpose: sharing the message of Jesus Christ and the hope of eternal life.

Even so, I never allowed my dreams to be extinguished. I went on to find success as a Coach, Manager, and Referee in rugby league — proving that when God closes one door, He opens another. The game I loved shaped me, but it was faith that defined me.

In this 'Life,' I've learned that we have to be prepared to lose everything to gain everything. Since Christmas Day 1979, I've been searching for answers and, by God's grace, I have found them. For over 46 years, I've listened, documented, and acknowledged things that have not yet been fully understood or implemented. I am not ashamed of my faith. Not only do you read, listen, and view the 'Truth,' but you are also witnessing what is happening and what will happen.

On the 16th of March 2001, I met Jesus in person. It was not a dream, not a vision — it was a real, personal encounter with the Lord Himself. That meeting changed everything and confirmed what I had long sensed: that God had a specific and extraordinary assignment for my life. But telling the truth has come at a cost. From the moment I began sharing what happened that day, I have faced discrimination — mocked, dismissed, and marginalised by people who could not or would not believe. That rejection has followed me my whole life. I carry it without bitterness, because I know what I saw, and I know Who I met.

Through that encounter and through years of seeking, listening, and documenting what the Spirit has revealed, I have come to understand — and I say this humbly but plainly — that I play a major part in the Rapture, the Caught-Up, and the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. I do not say this to exalt myself, but because God does not hide His plans from those He has appointed. "Surely the Lord God does nothing without revealing His secret to His servants the prophets." — Amos 3:7

Not long ago, in the most ordinary of places — a simple fruit shop — I met someone. A chance encounter, or so it seemed. But there is a reason for everything. Their name was that of the 13th Apostle — the apostle appointed after the original twelve, whose place is recorded in Scripture. I recognised it immediately. I will not reveal who they are. But if you know, you know. God does not arrange meetings by accident. To that person: you know who you are, and so does He.

I did not know what events would transpire from that meeting. It was not a foregone conclusion — none of it was planned by human hands. What has unfolded through that encounter, and through the processes that followed, is beyond anything I could have expected. I could never have done this without them. God placed the right person at the right time, and together we have been able to accomplish what I had been trying to do for years — getting this testimony out to the world.

On the 18th of April 2026, I rang Matthias — the thirteenth disciple. I was worried because he was going to a church the following morning that had not been speaking about the Rapture. I told him: we will know tomorrow — if the preacher does not bring up the Rapture, we will know it is not of the Spirit. On the 19th of April, Matthias rang me back and told me his preacher had spoken plainly about how desperate the hour is — the Rapture. All these churches out there — most of them are not talking about the Rapture at the moment. They must be.

What you are about to witness is a "Collusion of the highest Forces" between Good and bad. God being me "Righteous" and taking on more than what David did, but to convict the world. John 16:8 spells out your future! With this Scripture the Spirit of Truth — the Holy Spirit — convicts the world as humanity is in the "Fallen State." So I lead people who are searching for answers and wanting to be "Transformed" into the Image of Jesus Christ. You have to be in a "Personal Relationship" or you miss out on your inheritance! I "Unveil" God's plans for Redemption or Condemnation.

I am infiltrating the world with this book. Nothing can happen on any site, anywhere, without the implementation of God's truth. Deceitfulness shall be obscured, and righteousness shall be adhered to. Everything else must align with it. Anyone who stands in His way is gone.

God's Heroes — Benjamin Netanyahu & Donald Trump

Benjamin Netanyahu and Donald Trump are God's heroes. Benjamin — for over 40 years you have taken on all these lunatics. Donald Trump — God loves you. Everything you are doing has already been done. You are God's wild card. You will be raptured — caught up — out of this fallen world!

Thousands of years ago, in the Book of Esther, a young Jewish woman stood before the King of Persia to save her people from destruction. Haman — an official in the Persian court — had plotted to annihilate every Jew in the empire. Queen Esther risked her life to expose the plot and her people were saved. The Jews celebrated their deliverance and called it Purim — meaning "the lot" — because Haman had cast lots to choose the date of their destruction, and God turned it on its head. "For such a time as this" — Esther 4:14.

On the 28th of February 2026, that ancient story repeated itself. Benjamin Netanyahu and Donald Trump launched Operation Roaring Lion — joint strikes against Iran, the modern-day Persia. Netanyahu declared: "Today as well, on Purim, the lot has fallen, and the evil regime's end will also come. The prophet Amos said: 'The lion has roared — who will not fear?'" Jews around the world were on their knees praying. The prophecy about Benjamin was written thousands of years ago — and it played out on the same holy day, against the same enemy, in the same land. God does not deal in coincidence.

The Scriptures have declared all of this in advance. Isaiah 46:10 — "I make known the end from the beginning, from ancient times, what is still to come." Isaiah 54:17 — "No weapon formed against you shall prosper." Exodus 14:14 — "The Lord will fight for you; you need only to be still." Matthew 24:6-8 — "You will hear of wars and rumours of wars, but see to it that you are not alarmed. Such things must happen, but the end is still to come. Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom." John 16:8 — the Spirit of Truth convicts the world. Everything you are witnessing has already been written.

Jonathan Cahn is so of God. He has been revealing these mysteries for years. He prophesied October 7th, the night before — on Friday the 6th of October 2023, at Beth Israel in New Jersey, he shared a 3,000-year-old mystery from Leviticus that foretold an attack on Israel in the year 2023, in October, on a Sabbath. Before that night was over in America, Hamas invaded. Watch it here. And now Purim and Operation Roaring Lion have fulfilled the ancient pattern of Esther before our eyes — watch Jonathan Cahn reveal the mystery.

Through Wally's wife, I came to know someone whose husband used to be one of the people in control of WorkCover. He sat me down and told me straight: "Ed, you're just a number. It's all a game, and you've been played." And he was right.

For 14 months, I did not understand why the Lord had me in this position. But now I know — it was about my work injury with my knee and the way I was treated by WorkCover. I have caught them out.

On the 3rd of March 2025, I did an injury on the job as a traffic controller. But I kept working — two weeks straight, 12-hour shifts, sometimes 14 hours by the time you lace up your boots, get out on site, and get home again. I did not stop. By the 16th of March, my leg just didn't work any more. My last day working was in front of the Surfers Paradise Surf Club, taking out a lane in absolute agony. But you work, and you try to keep the payments up.

WorkCover kept me on the system for nine months, playing me with physiotherapy and psychology, but they gave me nothing. They were ready to scrub me altogether. The whole system — solicitors, insurers, the lot of them — they are all scammers, and they played me like a game.

But God had other plans. The same knee surgeon from the Mater who was ready to operate on me a year ago is now scheduled to give me a new knee. WorkCover, after throwing me under the bus, has been forced to take me back. My doctor has put me back on the system, and the surgery that should have happened 12 months ago is now going ahead. They tried to take me down, but everything coincides until the main factor takes control — and anyone who stands in His way is gone. "My God shall supply all your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus." — Philippians 4:19.

Welcome

Jesus is the Highway to Heaven

Jesus is the Highway to Heaven is a book for every person who has ever felt lost, afraid, or far from God. Through heartfelt testimony, scriptural truth, and personal reflection, Edwin Lewis shares the message that Jesus Christ is not merely a path — He is the path. No matter where life has taken you, no matter what you have been through, the highway home is open.

This ministry exists to point people to Jesus — to offer hope in hardship, clarity in confusion, and the assurance that God's love reaches further than any failure or fear. Whether you are a lifelong believer or simply searching, you are welcome here. Read, reflect, and walk this journey of faith at your own pace. God has a plan for you, and it begins with a single step toward Him.

As people listen to what is in front of them, aligning with this testimony, we will be able to do bigger miracles through Jesus' name than He was able to do in His day. God takes the glory through all of this. The only way you can get to Heaven is through Jesus. Even though we can do miracles, it is not for your glory — it is for God's glory. It is now the Holy Spirit's job to empower people, or not. You only have life as you allow Jesus to be your guide. It is only through Jesus' name that you will be able to do anything — until we are raptured, and then the antichrist is given his opportunity. God is not going to speak in parables any more; He will now speak in plain speech moving forward.
