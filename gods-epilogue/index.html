God's Epilogue

Cannon Hill Church Service

Pastor Don Shepherd

In this sermon, I hear Pastor Don draw on Habakkuk, Hosea 6, and Hebrews 12 to explain why the conditions for spiritual awakening are already present: sin and fear abound, the nation has forgotten God, and the church itself has become complacent. His message is not condemnation without hope. It is a call for us to return, repent, acknowledge the Lord, unite in prayer, and seek His presence.

I hear Don speaking directly to our church at Cannon Hill, warning us not to let our love disappear like the morning mist or become cold and indifferent to people outside God’s kingdom.

His warning to Cannon Hill was plain: return to God and reject complacency. The church did not listen. It moved away from God and, when I most needed fellowship and support, it abandoned me as well. For me, this recording preserves both Pastor Don’s call and the cost of the church’s refusal to listen.

The Lighthorse Anointing

This message uses the Australian Light Horse charge at Beersheba as a picture of courageous Christian mission. It calls believers to act with compassion, share God’s gifts without seeking personal glory, and carry His love and power into Australia and beyond.
