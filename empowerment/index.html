Messengers of Fire — JoAnn McFatter, Steve Mitchell & Steve Swanson

On the 20th of May, the Lord put this C.D. in my drawer, right in front of my computer. I had never seen it before — it was just there. As you listen to what is on this recording, you will be empowered by it. Just keep listening to it. And if you don't feel it — listen again.

This recording speaks of a storage place in Heaven — a place the Lord has been saving up, filled with revelation. It declares: "Go to that storage place. Isn't it time? You've been storing it for such a time as this. For now, we're so in need of revelation. Open the door, Lord. Not just a little crack — swing the door right open. Because the room is so full, Lord God, as soon as you open the door, it's gonna come tumbling out. It's gonna fall from Heaven to earth. Because, Lord, you've been saving it up for a time like this. For the desperate ones, for the hungry ones. Blessed are the desperate ones. Blessed are the hungry ones. Lord God, let it fall."

Track 6 in particular carries a powerful word — a revelation for everyone, the small, the great. God has been waiting for someone to ask.
